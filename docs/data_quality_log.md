# Data-quality log · Registro de calidad de datos

Findings noticed while validating the dashboard against GA4, each with the decision taken. / Hallazgos detectados al validar el dashboard contra GA4, cada uno con la decisión tomada.

Period / Periodo: September 1–30, 2026 · Source / Fuente: GA4 demo account (Google Merchandise Store)

| # | Finding (EN) | Hallazgo (ES) | Evidence / Evidencia | Decision / Decisión |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Channel rows add up to more sessions than the total. | Las filas de canales suman más sesiones que el total. | 140,051 vs. 137,067 (102.2%). Reproduced in Looker Studio, so it comes from GA4. | Documented; cause unconfirmed. Site-level figures use the GA4 total; channel rates use each channel's own row. / Documentado; causa sin confirmar. Las cifras del sitio usan el total de GA4; las tasas por canal usan la fila de cada canal. |
| 2 | Device rows add up to more sessions than the total. | Las filas de dispositivo suman más sesiones que el total. | 137,980 vs. 137,067 (+0.7%). | Documented; cause unconfirmed. / Documentado; causa sin confirmar. |
| 3 | Looker Studio offers several channel dimensions with almost identical names, and they do not give the same numbers. | Looker Studio ofrece varias dimensiones de canal con nombres casi iguales que no dan los mismos números. | Only "Default channel group (session)" matched GA4 row by row (10 of 10 rows). | Used that dimension; validated every figure against a known GA4 number. / Se usó esa dimensión; se validó cada cifra contra un número conocido de GA4. |
| 4 | Sessions without an identified country ("not set") generate no purchases. | Las sesiones sin país identificado ("not set") no generan compras. | 43,403 sessions (31.7%), 0 purchases, $0; 98.2% of them are Direct + mobile; they barely view products. | Excluded when comparing devices and countries; reported separately with a note on the page. Without them, purchases per session rise from 1.40% to ≈ 2.05%. / Excluidas al comparar dispositivos y países; reportadas aparte con una nota en la página. Sin ellas, las compras por sesión suben de 1,40% a ≈ 2,05%. |
| 5 | "Direct" is a large share of sessions and its origin is unknown. | "Direct" es una gran parte de las sesiones y su origen es desconocido. | ≈ 61% of sessions. It includes untagged links, apps, emails and automated traffic, not only typed addresses. | Never read as "people typing the address"; stated as a limitation. / Nunca se lee como "personas que escriben la dirección"; se declara como limitación. |
| 6 | Possible internal Google traffic. | Posible tráfico interno de Google. | Top cities include Mountain View, Sunnyvale, San Jose and Tokyo. | Not removable with the demo data; stated as a limitation. / No se puede quitar con los datos de la demo; se declara como limitación. |
| 7 | Unassigned shows an inconsistent pattern. | Unassigned muestra un patrón inconsistente. | Key-event rate (23.83%) higher than engagement rate (13.05%). | Cause unconfirmed; recommendation 1b asks to review link tagging. / Causa sin confirmar; la recomendación 1b pide revisar el etiquetado de enlaces. |
| 8 | The demo account rounds figures, and GA4 warns that behavior metrics may be modeled (consent mode). | La cuenta demo redondea cifras, y GA4 avisa que las métricas de comportamiento pueden estar modeladas (modo de consentimiento). | GA4 notice; rounded values. | Stated in the executive summary. / Declarado en el resumen ejecutivo. |
| 9 | Key events are not purchases. | Los eventos clave no son compras. | 159,380 key events vs. 1,921 purchases (key events include add to cart, begin checkout, etc.). | Always use the purchase event for purchases. / Siempre se usa el evento de compra para las compras. |
| 10 | Open vs. closed funnel give slightly different numbers. | El embudo abierto y el cerrado dan números ligeramente distintos. | Start: 102,614 (Looker) vs. 102,086 (GA4). Purchases: 1,773 vs. 1,715. The order of the losses is the same. | Both reported; the dashboard uses the open funnel (users per event). / Se reportan ambos; el dashboard usa el embudo abierto (usuarios por evento). |
| 11 | Users are not additive across devices. | Los usuarios no se suman entre dispositivos. | Mobile + desktop + tablet = 103,328 vs. 102,614 total (same user on several devices). | Note on the funnel page; only purchases add up exactly. / Nota en la página del embudo; solo las compras suman exacto. |
| 12 | "Purchases per session" is not GA4's purchase rate. | "Compras por sesión" no es la tasa de compra de GA4. | Our calculated field = purchases ÷ sessions = 1.40%; GA4's rate (sessions with a purchase ÷ sessions) = 1.38%. | Used our calculated field consistently and named it explicitly. / Se usó nuestro campo calculado de forma consistente y con nombre explícito. |
| 13 | Small samples. | Muestras pequeñas. | Channels, countries and devices under 1,000 sessions (e.g. tablet 975 sessions and 4 purchases; smart tv 1 session). | Not used for recommendations; this is my own judgment-based threshold, not an exact rule. / No se usan para recomendar; es un umbral de juicio propio, no una regla exacta. |
| 14 | No advertising or product cost data. | No hay datos de costos de publicidad ni de productos. | Demo account. | Revenue is never presented as profit; profitability is not assessed. / Nunca se presenta ingresos como ganancia; no se evalúa rentabilidad. |
| 15 | Only one month of data. | Solo un mes de datos. | A spike in users around September 28–29 was not investigated. | Seasonality and trends not claimed; listed as a next step. / No se afirma estacionalidad ni tendencias; queda como paso siguiente. |
| 16 | The channel dropdown on page 2 of the English report shows its Spanish field name. | El selector de canal de la página 2 del informe en inglés muestra su nombre de campo en español. | Looker Studio keeps the name stored in the data source, and controls cannot be renamed. | Accepted; documented here. / Aceptado; documentado aquí. |
